Page 02 • Lesson 1

Learn Chinese through a passage

The easiest way to begin is to read a short conversation, understand who is speaking, and connect each Chinese sentence with its pinyin and English meaning.

Dialogue 1 — Welcome to Taiwan!

Read the Chinese line first, then use the pinyin to practise saying it aloud.

明華 Mínghuá: 請問你是陳月美小姐嗎？

Qǐngwèn nǐ shì Chén Yuèměi xiǎojiě ma?

Excuse me, are you Miss Chen Yuemei?

月美 Yuèměi: 是的。謝謝你來接我們。

Shì de. Xièxie nǐ lái jiē wǒmen.

Yes. Thank you for coming to pick us up.

明華 Mínghuá: 不客氣。我是李明華。

Bú kèqi. Wǒ shì Lǐ Mínghuá.

You're welcome. My name is Li Minghua.

月美 Yuèměi: 這是王先生。

Zhè shì Wáng xiānsheng.

This is Mr. Wang.

凱文 Kǎiwén: 你好。我姓王，叫凱文。

Nǐ hǎo. Wǒ xìng Wáng, jiào Kǎiwén.

Hi. My surname is Wang. My first name is Kaiwen.

明華 Mínghuá: 你們好。歡迎你們來臺灣。

Nǐmen hǎo. Huānyíng nǐmen lái Táiwān.

How are you? Welcome to Taiwan.

People in the dialogue

NameWho?
陳月美
Chén Yuèměi		A woman from Vietnam
李明華
Lǐ Mínghuá		A man from Taiwan
王凱文
Wáng Kǎiwén		A man from the US
Remember: In Chinese names, the family name (surname) usually comes before the given name. For example, Wáng is the surname and Kǎiwén is the given name.
